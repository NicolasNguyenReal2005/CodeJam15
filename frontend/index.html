HOW IT WORKS

From debate to insight in seconds

  • 1

    Read any article or newspaper

    Open any article (or newspaper of your choice!) and start reading.

  • 2

    Click “Analyze”

    Paste in any text that arouses suspicion, click analyse and NOBUL will take care of slicing any uncertainties apart.

  • 3

    See fallacies and counter-examples

    Get clear labels like “Strawman” or “Ad hominem”, plus short explanations and counter-examples to help you respond or think more critically.
