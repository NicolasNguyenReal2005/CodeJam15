HOW IT WORKS

From debate to insight in seconds

  • 1

    Play any debate or video

    Open YouTube (or your video platform of choice), start the debate, and let NOBUL quietly listen in the background.

  • 2

    Click “Analyze last 2 minutes”

    Tell NOBUL when to analyze. It grabs the recent transcript and looks for common logical fallacies and weak reasoning.

  • 3

    See fallacies and counter-examples

    Get clear labels like “Strawman” or “Ad hominem”, plus short explanations and counter-examples to help you respond or think more critically.
